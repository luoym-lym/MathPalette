# Sources and licenses

Retained questions keep `source_dataset` and available `source_key` values. Counts: DynaMath 20, MathVerse 23, MathVista 9, We-Math2.0-Standard 25, Author Designed 145, and MathVision 101.

MathPalette redraws and Special variants do not imply endorsement by upstream authors. No single new license is asserted for all sources.

- **MathVision:** [MathLLMs/MathVision](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MathLLMs/MathVision), pinned data revision `2837ddb3f13abaf6b3997c12d80753e5470bd46a`. Ke Wang et al., *Measuring Multimodal Mathematical Reasoning with MATH-Vision Dataset*, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks 2024. The original copyright notice and [MIT license](attribution/MathVision-MIT.txt) are retained.
- **MathVista:** [AI4Math/MathVista testmini](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI4Math/MathVista). Pan Lu et al., *MathVista: Evaluating Mathematical Reasoning of Foundation Models in Visual Contexts*, ICLR 2024. The retained upstream notice identifies new contributions as CC BY-SA 4.0, with original questions and images belonging to their authors. Subsources may have separate terms; the original notice identifies test-set use restrictions and IconQA as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Original MIT notices for [InterGPS](attribution/InterGPS-MIT.txt) and [TheoremQA](attribution/TheoremQA-MIT.txt) are retained.
- **We-Math2.0-Standard:** [We-Math/We-Math2.0-Standard](https://huggingface.co/datasets/We-Math/We-Math2.0-Standard), pinned revision `19064a4b3be262db1fadc952d03b3102acccf942`. Runqi Qiao et al., *We-Math 2.0: A Versatile MathBook System for Incentivizing Visual Mathematical Reasoning*, 2025, [arXiv:2508.10433](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10433). The [CC BY-NC 4.0 license](attribution/CC-BY-NC-4.0.txt) is retained.
- **DynaMath, MathVerse, and Author Designed:** existing source names and identifiers are retained. This cleanup does not assert additional rights.

License texts in `attribution/` are unchanged. Historical import reports and source screenshots are not evaluation inputs.
