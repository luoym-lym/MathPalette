1.  **Analyze the Base:** All options show a strip of four squares. In a cube net, this strip forms the lateral sides of the cube. The four triangles attached to this strip must fold over to form the top and bottom faces (lids).

2.  **Analyze the Fold Geometry:**
    *   When a triangular flap folds 90 degrees to form a lid, the hypotenuse (the long diagonal side) becomes a vertical edge shared with the adjacent side of the cube.
    *   Therefore, for the lid to close properly, the triangular flaps attached to the **same side** of the strip (e.g., both on top) must alternate in orientation ("handedness"). If two adjacent flaps on the same side are oriented the same way (e.g., both slanted the same direction like `/` and `/`), their hypotenuses will end up parallel and separated by a gap, or they will overlap in a way that leaves part of the cube open.
    *   Correct patterns must be alternating, like `/` `\` `/` `\` or `\` `/` `\` `/`.

3.  **Evaluate the Options:**
    *   **A:** The triangles alternate in orientation on the top and bottom. This is a valid "alternating" pattern.
    *   **B:** The triangles alternate in orientation on the top and bottom. This is valid.
    *   **C:** The triangles alternate in orientation on the top and bottom. This is valid.
    *   **D:** This net has two triangles on the **top** side of the strip. Looking at the vertical edges highlighted in red, the first triangle has its vertical edge on the left (orientation `/`), and the second triangle also has its vertical edge on the left (orientation `/`). Because these two triangles have the **same orientation**, they are not alternating. When folded, they will not cover the square ends of the cube; instead, there will be a triangular gap on one side and overlapping on the other.

    *   The first square (left) folds up to be the **Back** face. The triangle attached to it folds to become part of the **Left** face.
    *   The third square (right) folds up to be the **Front** face. The triangle attached to it folds to become part of the **Top** face.
    *   Now look at the second triangle on the bottom strip (attached to the middle square). It folds up to cover the **Left** face as well.
    *   **Collision**: The triangle from the Back face (D) and the triangle from the Bottom face both attempt to cover the same spot on the Left face of the cube.
    *   Because the flaps overlap, this net leaves the **Right** face of the cube completely open.

3.  **Conclusion**: Since option D results in overlapping flaps and an open side, it cannot form a closed cube.
