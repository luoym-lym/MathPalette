# Solution

Measure directed angles counterclockwise from AB (to the right). Reflection in a line of direction t sends a direction d to 2t−d modulo 360°. A line and the same line with its direction reversed give the same reflection.

Before the first fold, the upper arm has direction α; the lower arm has direction 180°+α. Reflecting the upper arm across AB changes its direction to 360°−α.

The second crease has down-left direction 180°+γ=225°. Thus r=2(180°+γ)−(360°−α)=α+2γ=164°.

The last crease is parallel to the first-fold right arm, hence has down-right direction 360°−α=286°. The reflected original left arm has direction l=2(360°−α)−(180°+α)=540°−3α=318°.

The indicated long edges, with the explicitly drawn backward extensions where needed, define the two rays at the marked vertex. Therefore β is the smaller angle between r=164° and l=318°, giving **β=154°**, choice **A**. This derives the result from the fold rules, rather than measuring the final picture.

The other options cannot satisfy the same reflection equations:
- B: 148° is 2α and omits the independent second-crease inclination.
- C: 90° is 2γ and omits both the initial obliquity and the final arm reflection.
- D: 26° is the supplement, using the backward ray of one long edge.
- E: 96° is the parent edge-parallel rule 6(90°−α), which does not apply to this specified second crease.

The English conditions specify each moving arm, its end portion, and the directed inclination of every non-parallel crease. The horizontal-image rule, where used, also specifies that the image points left. Thus supplementary angle-bisector axes and the opposite moving half-plane are excluded by the stated rules. Translation, strip width, and arm lengths cannot alter directions or this angle. The independent checker reconstructs the material, verifies area and distances, and proves that both marked rays lie on actual long-edge lines with every needed extension explicitly drawn. A sector may lie over a paper layer; it is still the marked angle between the two specified edges.
