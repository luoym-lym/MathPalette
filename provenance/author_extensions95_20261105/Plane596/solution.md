Normalize the large squares to side length 1.

In I, the full triangle below the diagonal has area 1/2. Its part in the middle vertical third is a trapezoid with parallel vertical sides 1/3 and 2/3 and separation 1/3. That unshaded part has area ((1/3 + 2/3)/2)(1/3) = 1/6. Hence the shaded area is 1/2 - 1/6 = 1/3. Equivalently, the two shaded pieces have areas 1/18 and 5/18.

In II, a horizontal section of the large triangle at height y above its base has width 1-y. The shaded trapezoid lies between heights 1/3 and 2/3; its two parallel sides have lengths 2/3 and 1/3. Its area is ((2/3 + 1/3)/2)(1/3) = 1/6.

In III, three of the nine equal grid squares are shaded, giving 3/9 = 1/3. Thus S_I = S_III = 1/3 > S_II = 1/6. The answer is D.
