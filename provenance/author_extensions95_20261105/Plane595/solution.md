Normalize every large square to side length 1.

In I, joining the side midpoints gives a diamond of area 1/2. Joining its side midpoints gives a square of area 1/4 (its side length is 1/2). The four shaded triangles between these boundaries therefore have total area 1/2 - 1/4 = 1/4.

In II, subtract the three unshaded corner triangles from the unit square. Their areas are 1/4, 1/4 and 1/8, so the shaded triangle has area 1 - 1/4 - 1/4 - 1/8 = 3/8.

In III, the shaded triangle has base 1 and height 1, so its area is 1/2. Thus S_I = 1/4 < S_II = 3/8 < S_III = 1/2. The answer is B.
