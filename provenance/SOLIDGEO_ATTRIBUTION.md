The original review batch contained 168 source examples. The current formal collection retains 9 user-approved examples (Solid17–Solid25); the other 159 candidates were removed. Source images and source/provenance records for the retained questions are preserved. The retained IDs and cleanup verification are recorded in [solidgeo_cleanup.json](solidgeo_cleanup.json). Changes include code-based redraws, removal of decorative rendering, canonical source/answer metadata, explicit choice mappings, relocation of question/answer fields out of frozen scenes, and a documented current-environment rendering migration. Per-question provenance describes the transformations and preserves the distinction between upstream answers, independently audited answers, and user approval. No candidate is formally approved merely by appearing here.
